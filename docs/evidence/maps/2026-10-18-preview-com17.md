## Bounded tests actually executed

Identity-checked `.local/maps/com17_label_bench.py` ran from integration worktree
with preview-normal-plan-20261018.json, preview-reclaim-plan-20261018.json and
preview-sd-plan-20261018.json. Output logs share those prefixes without `plan`.

- Normal: six pan/zoom actions all completed while receiving Shortwave at
  14.070 MHz. Map holder reports 2,835,456 bytes (two 1,417,728-byte buffers).
- Reclaim: after leaving picker and stopping RF, reclaimed map holder reports
  zero. A bounded PSRAM hog held 19,660,800 bytes, leaving approximately 2.6 MB.
  Map still reached ready with only a 1,417,728-byte holder: snapshot allocation
  safely fell back rather than preventing final rendering. No reset/hang.
- After releasing hog/frame memory, holder again zero; normal memory restored,
  RF resumed, and entering picker recreated both buffers (2,835,456 bytes).
- Retained-buffer SD: unique 8 KiB scratch uploaded/read back identically,
  SHA-256 dc404a613fedaeb54034514bc6505f56b933caa5250299ba7d094377a51caa46.
  Holder remained 2,835,456 bytes. RF was paused for bulk transfer as required.
  Scratch remains `/orcsdr/maplabels_330606cb_preview_20261018.bin`.
- Final stream approximately 2.399 MSPS, driver drops=0/overruns=0. No observed
  reboot after intentional flash reset. Final DMA free 11,215, largest 10,752,
  minimum 8,999; internal free 33,219, minimum 9,847; PSRAM free 19,174,232.
  Total minimum heap was deliberately lowered by fault injection and must not
  be called an organic memory regression.

## Physical acceptance

After tests left COM17 on the map picker while receiving, user answered
**"Moves with finger; noticeably better"** to drag/zoom responsiveness check.
This establishes useful immediate cached-motion feedback on that setup.
It does not establish 33 ms/p95 latency, optimal transfer performance, all
zoom/world-copy cases or long-term stability. Existing raw frame intervals
include dwell and scripted pauses and cannot substitute for gesture latency.

Remaining: measure preview push/input-to-visible timing and continuous-motion
percentiles, check labels/overlays across all cameras, optimize measured costs,
finish worldwide boundary/font quality, trusted catalog/download and setup.
No main merge or release occurred; overall plan remains incomplete.

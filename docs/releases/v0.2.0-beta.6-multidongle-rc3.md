# OrcSDR v0.2.0-beta.6 Multi-Dongle RC3

RC3 is an experimental prerelease for testing. It replaces RC2 for
multi-dongle testing, but the work remains unmerged and is not a stable
OrcSDR release.

## Important RC2 correction

The published RC2 image pinned older `esp-rtl-sdr` commit
`8cc5bbbc3986b15ca28223df167abd8ccfce30d5`. RC3 pins the tested driver
commit `26e3857cb6a2cc7c09a7197ce07c46c29becd4e0`, which includes the USB
transfer drain repair described below.

## Downloads

- `OrcSDR-Tab5-v0.2.0-beta.6-multidongle-rc3.bin` is the complete merged
  image for M5Burner at its default address.
- `OrcSDR-Tab5-v0.2.0-beta.6-multidongle-rc3-native-app-0x10000.bin` is the
  application-only ESP-IDF image for address `0x10000`.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the M5Burner image hash. The native application
  hash is listed below.

The local M5Burner ZIP is not attached because its Linux `flash.sh`
line-ending problem remains open in issue #65. This does not affect the
merged M5Burner `.bin`.

## What changed since RC2

- Pins `esp-rtl-sdr` commit `26e3857cb6a2cc7c09a7197ce07c46c29becd4e0`.
- Prevents the driver from reusing USB transfers still owned by the USB host
  after a drain timeout.
- Reworks AM station scanning to retune through all 119 channels at the normal
  sample rate instead of allocating a large wideband capture.
- Always shows the AM scan save/discard result prompt, including when zero
  stations are found, then restores the previous AM frequency and audio.
- Caps RF analysis to the IQ samples actually present, preventing an
  out-of-bounds read and task-stack corruption.
- Aligns the connected Wi-Fi regression with the supported radio-pause path.

## Blog V4 acceptance

The exact packaged RC3 image was installed and tested with an RTL-SDR Blog
V4. The driver regression, on-device UI regression, five-cycle dashboard
smoke test, and ten-cycle radio scan soak passed. AM scan completed 119 of
119 channels, showed the result prompt, restored 1.280 MHz and audio, and
reported zero driver USB overruns and zero consumer drops. Bias-T remained
off.

During the AM scan test, the small dipole then connected to the radio produced
zero station candidates. That result confirms the scan completed safely; it
does not measure AM sensitivity. Antenna choice can affect what is received,
but OrcSDR does not require a particular antenna type. Reception reports are
more useful when they include the antenna used.

## Provisional receivers

- RTL-SDR Blog V3 remains experimental.
- Nooelec NESDR SMArt V5 remains experimental.
- Both may stream only at 24 MHz or above unless their reported capabilities
  say otherwise. Unsupported HF and controls remain blocked.
- Build success is not reception acceptance.

## Other known limits

- LoRa reception at 906.875 MHz, SF11, BW250k improved and decoded controlled
  messages and natural telemetry, but intermittent missed traffic and long
  processing of difficult CRC-failed captures still need work.
- C6 unknown-version recovery in issue #82 remains separate work. RC3 does
  not claim that issue is fixed.
- Bias-T was not tested in this round.
- The unfinished AM/FM/shortwave station-database generators, scraped station
  data, and catalog experiments are intentionally not included.

## C6 firmware

Every RC3 release image contains the matching ESP-Hosted C6 3.0.6 firmware
inside the Tab5 application. The release validator found the complete C6
image embedded byte-for-byte. If the internal C6 needs repair or updating,
use the existing confirmed update/reinstall flow in Settings > Firmware &
Updates.

## What testers should report

For V3 or Nooelec testing, please include the exact receiver model,
installation method, frequency and band, antenna, whether the waterfall
moved, whether audio or a known signal was received, unplug/replug behavior,
and a complete serial log from cold boot through the test or failure.
